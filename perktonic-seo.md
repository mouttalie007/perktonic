## 3. Technical SEO Checklist

- Permalink structure: `/%postname%/`, categories without the `/category/` base if possible.
- Each article: title ≤ 60 chars, meta ≤ 155 chars, one H1, keyword in the first 100 words, 3 to 5 internal links, 1 to 2 authoritative external links.
- Schema: `WebSite` + `Organization` on Home, `Article` + `Author` on posts, `FAQPage` on how-to guides, `BreadcrumbList` everywhere.
- Add "Last updated" date plus author box (real name, short bio) to every article.
- Submit `sitemap.xml` to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools from day one.
- Core Web Vitals: lightweight theme, WebP images, lazy loading, caching plugin.
- Pinterest: a pin-ready vertical image (1000×1500) in every article.

## 5. Cookie Consent Banner (GDPR / ePrivacy / Google requirement)

Google requires a **certified Consent Management Platform (CMP)** for AdSense traffic from the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Set it up before applying and before going live with ads.

**Recommended options (pick one):**
1. **Google's own consent message** (AdSense → Privacy & messaging). Free and integrated.
2. **Complianz** or **CookieYes** (WordPress plugins with Google Consent Mode v2 support).

**Configuration checklist:**
- Banner with three equal-weight buttons: **Accept all**, **Reject all**, **Manage preferences**. Rejecting must be as easy as accepting.
- No analytics or ad cookies before consent (for EEA/UK visitors).
- Enable **Google Consent Mode v2**.
- Persistent **"Cookie settings"** link in the footer to change choices.
- Link the banner to the [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).
- Log consent (the plugin does this).

**Banner text (template):**
> **We value your privacy.** We use cookies to make the site work, understand traffic and show ads. You can accept all, reject non-essential cookies, or choose your settings. Read our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/).
> **[Accept all]  [Reject all]  [Manage preferences]**
