## Hero
Paste a report, article or set of minutes, or open a Word file. This ai document summarizer online gives you a summary, 3 to 10 key points with the sentence each one comes from, and a check of every number.

## How to use
1. Paste the text (200 to 20,000 characters), or open a .txt, .md or .docx file. The file is read in your browser.
2. Choose the length: short (about 3 sentences), medium (one paragraph) or long (two paragraphs).
3. Choose the tone: executive, technical or simple. Tick "Add a section-by-section digest" if the document has several parts.
4. Press **Summarise** and read the result. Any figure that we could not find in your text is highlighted in amber.
5. Copy the summary, or download it as Markdown or plain text.

## Examples
**A library's annual review** (about 400 words, medium, executive):
- Summary: "The Riverside Library Network had 412,000 visitors in 2025, up from 368,000 in 2024, mainly thanks to two branches that opened until 9 pm on weekdays. Digital loans rose 22% to 156,000…"
- Key point: "A $350,000 city grant funded self-service kiosks in 5 branches." It comes with its source quote from the review.

**A load-test report** (short, simple tone): three plain sentences on how many orders the checkout handled and where it slowed down. The latency figures are kept exactly as written.

**French council minutes** (short): the summary stays in French: "Le conseil a adopté le budget rectifié de la piscine…". Figures such as "1,2 million d'euros" are recognised with French separators.

## Tips and limits
- **Figures are checked, not trusted.** We compare every number in the summary with the numbers in your text. "12.5%" matches "12,5 %" and "$1,250,000" matches "1 250 000". A number that is missing is highlighted and counted, never silently removed.
- **Quotes are checked too.** A key point whose quote cannot be found in your text is labelled "not verified".
- The limit is 20,000 characters, about 3,000 words or 8 to 10 pages. This version does not split long documents: summarise them in parts.
- **PDF is not supported yet.** Open the PDF, select all, copy, and paste the text here. Old .doc files must be saved again as .docx.
- The summary is written in the language of the document.
- A summary leaves things out by design. For decisions, read the parts that matter in full.

## FAQ
**How do I summarise a long PDF?**
Copy the text out of the PDF and paste it into the box. If it is longer than 20,000 characters, paste one chapter or section at a time and summarise each one.

**Does it keep important numbers?**
The AI is asked to keep key figures exactly as written. We then check every number in the summary against your text and highlight any we cannot find.

**What is the maximum length?**
20,000 characters per request, and files up to 5 MB. Longer text is refused with a message; it is never cut silently.

**Can it summarise Word documents?**
Yes. A .docx file is converted to text in your browser, and only that text is sent when you press Summarise.

**What does the section-by-section digest do?**
It adds one or two sentences for each heading or part of the document, in the order they appear. This is useful for reports and minutes.

**Which languages work?**
English works best. French, Spanish and other major languages are summarised in their own language.

**How many summaries can I make?**
10 AI requests per 10 minutes. There may also be a daily limit, and the page tells you when you reach it.

## Privacy note
When you press Summarise, the text is sent to our server and to the AI provider to write the summary. Files are read in your browser first, and only their text is sent. We do not store the text with your identity. An identical request may be answered from a short-lived cache. Nothing is sent when the page opens: the example result was saved in advance.
