## Hero
Describe a process in a few fields. This sop template generator online builds a complete document with purpose, scope, roles, steps, KPIs and a revision history. You edit it on the page and export it to Word, Markdown or PDF.

## How to use
1. Choose the document type: SOP, policy, onboarding checklist or process documentation.
2. Fill in the process name, industry and team size (a number or a range such as 5-10). Optionally add your key steps, one per line, and the owner.
3. Press **Generate template**. The document is built in your browser and nothing is sent. Every gap shows "[to be completed]".
4. Optionally, press **Improve with AI** to have the gaps filled in. Your own steps are kept as you wrote them.
5. Edit any section: add, remove or reorder steps, roles, KPIs and revisions.
6. Export with **Download Word (.docx)**, **Download Markdown** or **Print / Save as PDF**, or copy it.

## Examples
**Customer refund requests** (SOP, e-commerce, 8 people, 4 steps given, owner Maria Lopez). Improve with AI keeps the four steps word for word and adds four more, such as "Log the refund request in the support inbox". It assigns an owner to each step and proposes 3 KPIs, such as "Time to decision", with targets left as "[to be completed]". The revision history reads 1.0, today's date, Maria Lopez, "Initial draft".

**Remote work** (policy, software, 40-60 people, no steps). The template alone gives the six sections with placeholders. With AI, the steps cover agreeing a pattern, equipment and security. The number of remote days stays "[to be completed]" because you did not give one.

**Chemical spill response** (SOP, manufacturing, 25 people). Your four steps stay in order. Safety points, such as checking the safety data sheet for protective equipment, appear as steps and as notes. No regulation is named, because you did not name one.

## Tips and limits
- **No invented regulations.** If the AI cites a law or standard you did not write, such as an ISO number, GDPR, OSHA or "Section 12", that sentence is removed and the page tells you. If you name one yourself, it stays.
- **No invented figures, dates or company names.** A sentence with a number that is not in your brief is removed and listed, so targets stay as placeholders for you to set.
- Steps are numbered by the page, and renumbered whenever you move or delete one.
- Key steps sent to the AI are limited to 2,000 characters. The template itself has no limit and lists up to 30 steps.
- The PDF comes from your browser's print dialog: choose "Save as PDF".
- The AI writes in English.

## FAQ
**What is an SOP?**
A standard operating procedure is a written, step-by-step description of how a recurring task is done. It says who does each step and how success is measured, so the work is done the same way every time.

**Can I edit the document?**
Yes. Every section is an editable field before export. You can add, remove and reorder steps, roles, KPIs and revision rows.

**Which sections does it include?**
Always six, in this order: Purpose, Scope, Roles and responsibilities, Steps, KPIs, and Revision history.

**Do I need the AI?**
No. Generate template works offline in your browser. The AI only fills gaps and suggests wording.

**Will it tell me which laws apply?**
No. It removes regulation references you did not give, because a wrong citation is worse than none. Check the rules that apply to your organisation with a qualified person.

**Can I export to Word?**
Yes. The .docx file is created in your browser and keeps the headings and tables.

## Privacy note
Generate template runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing. When you press Improve with AI, the form fields are sent to our server and to the AI provider to draft the document. We do not store them with your identity. An identical request may be answered from a short-lived cache. Edits and exports happen in your browser.
