40 unique article briefs (articles 1-20 are the previous file's 13-32 renumbered, 21-40 are new), each with a full writing prompt (article brief, editorial line, hook rules, advanced SEO and technical spec, research-backed external links, and a strict output format). How to use this file
  1. Pick a card. Click Copy full prompt. Everything for that article is now on your clipboard: brief, keywords, voice, structure, SEO spec, output format.
  2. Paste it into an AI writer that can browse the web: the prompt makes it research and add 3-6 real external links. Review the draft like an editor, and open every external link before publishing.
  3. There is no word target: the writer sets the length by the topic, with no padding and no thin sections (a soft sanity floor of about 700 words applies for AdSense quality).
  4. The card turns green and is remembered in this browser. Use Mark as not done to undo, or Reset to clear everything.
  5. The AI replies in a fixed, publish-ready format: Title, Slug, Meta Description, Keywords, Category, Author (the last two are pre-filled for each article by this file), a --- line, then the HTML body with [IMAGE: ...] tags where each AI-generated image goes.
  6. Cards marked First-hand testing required have a notes box. Paste your real test notes there before copying. If it stays empty, the AI is told not to claim any personal testing and to write a research-based version.
  7. Each card has a Cover image box with its own scene and a Copy cover image prompt button: a magazine-style, non-AI-looking photo prompt for the featured image shown at the top of the article and in lists (3:2, centre-safe for square thumbnails).
  8. Two image tags: [IMAGE: ...] is an AI photo prompt written for ChatGPT (3:2 landscape); [IMAGE: SCREENSHOT: ...] is a note for you to capture by hand. The card lists the screenshot candidates in advance.
  9. If a ChatGPT image still looks polished, reply with: Make it more candid: flatter light, tighter crop, add everyday clutter, less colour grading.
  10. If your chat app swallows the HTML tags and shows only the header lines, tick Wrap reply in a code block at the top before copying the prompt.
  11. Keyword demand is not measured here. Check volume and difficulty for each primary keyword in Google Keyword Planner, Semrush or Ahrefs and adjust before you write.
